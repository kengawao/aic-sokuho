国内 2026-10-01 • Source: 25年度の介護費用 高齢化で最大に

スポンサーリンク

Japan's long-term care expenses are projected to reach an all-time high in fiscal 2025 as the nation's aging population accelerates. The surge is driven by increased demand for care services and expanded wage improvement allowances for care workers to address staffing shortages. Both the sustainability of the social security system and household preparation for care costs have become pressing concerns.

Key Points

  • Rising elderly population increases care service users and pushes total care spending to unprecedented levels
  • Expanded wage improvement subsidies for care workers likely to increase insurance premiums and out-of-pocket costs for users
  • Household financial planning for elderly care and the long-term viability of public welfare systems are critical issues
