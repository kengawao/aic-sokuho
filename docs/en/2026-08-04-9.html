Japan's Government Eyes Food Tax Cut to 1% from April 2025 - What Does This Mean for Your Wallet?

The Japanese government plans to decide by December 5th on a basic policy to reduce the consumption tax on food products to 1% for two years starting April 2025. The ruling Liberal Democratic Party's joint conference delegated the matter to Tax Commission Chairman Onodéra for coordination. This measure aims to ease the financial burden on households through a temporary tax reduction on essential food items.

Key Points

  • The 1% consumption tax rate on food is a temporary two-year measure with specific scope still being determined
  • April 2025 implementation is the target, with cabinet approval scheduled for December 5th
  • Detailed implementation methods and the exact definition of covered food items remain under negotiation
