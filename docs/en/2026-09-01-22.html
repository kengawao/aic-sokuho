Japan's Long-Term Interest Rates Hit 30-Year High: What Does It Mean for Your Wallet?

経済 2026-09-01 • Source: 長期金利 2.955％に上昇 約29年11か月ぶりの水準

スポンサーリンク

Japanese bond markets saw the 10-year government bond yield surge to 2.955%, reaching its highest level since October 1996—nearly 30 years ago. This reflects the Bank of Japan's shift toward monetary normalization and global interest rate pressures. The rise is expected to have significant ripple effects on household finances, including changes to mortgage rates and savings account returns.

Key Points

  • 10-year JGB yields reached 2.955%, the highest in approximately 29 years and 11 months
  • BOJ's monetary policy normalization and inflation responses are driving the rate increase
  • Higher rates will likely impact mortgage lending rates and household savings returns
