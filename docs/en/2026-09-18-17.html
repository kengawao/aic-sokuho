スポンサーリンク

San Marc Holdings, the parent company of San Marc Cafe bakery chain, has acquired Tsurutontan, a popular Sanuki udon noodle restaurant chain. The acquisition marks the company's strategic expansion into a new food service category, signaling broader industry consolidation trends in Japan's restaurant sector.

Key Points

  • San Marc Holdings is diversifying from bakery operations into the udon restaurant category to access new customer segments and mitigate business risk
  • Tsurutontan operates primarily in the Kansai region; San Marc's capital and network could enable nationwide expansion of the brand
  • The acquisition reflects accelerating industry consolidation and multi-format strategies among major Japanese restaurant chains
