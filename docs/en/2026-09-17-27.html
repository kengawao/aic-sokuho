The Bank of Japan is holding a monetary policy meeting starting September 17th and is expected to finalize a decision to raise the policy interest rate to around 1.25% on the 18th. This decision reflects heightened concerns about rising inflation risks and signals an acceleration in the pace of rate hikes. The rate increase will lead to higher deposit interest rates for savers but also increase borrowing costs for mortgages and businesses.

Key Points

  • Policy rate to rise to approximately 1.25%, demonstrating stronger stance against inflation risks
  • Rate hikes will have dual effects: increased savings returns but also higher borrowing costs for households and corporations
  • Potential spillover effects on corporate investment decisions and consumer spending patterns
