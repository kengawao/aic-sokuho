Generator Safety: Why Carbon Monoxide Poisoning is a Hidden Winter Risk

健康 2026-08-20 • Source: 発電機で一酸化炭素中毒 注意点

スポンサーリンク

Portable generators, essential during power outages and disasters, pose a serious carbon monoxide poisoning risk when used indoors. Cases of accidents have increased, particularly when generators are placed inside homes or enclosed spaces for heating during winter. Experts emphasize the critical need to understand proper usage guidelines and safety precautions before operating these devices.

Key Points

  • Carbon monoxide from generators is odorless and colorless, making it undetectable; even small amounts can cause poisoning with prolonged exposure
  • Generators must never be operated in enclosed or semi-enclosed spaces such as basements, tents, garages, or vehicles
  • Pre-disaster education and reviewing instruction manuals can save lives; proper knowledge is essential during emergencies when panic may cloud judgment
