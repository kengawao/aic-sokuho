COVID-19 Home Isolation for 18 Months Leads to Worker's Death Recognized as Workplace Injury

国内 2026-09-22 • Source: コロナ自宅待機1年半で自死 労災

スポンサーリンク

A worker who died by suicide after spending approximately 18 months in mandatory home isolation due to COVID-19 infection has been recognized as a workplace-related death. The case highlights the severe psychological toll of prolonged isolation and raises critical questions about balancing infection prevention with workers' mental health and wellbeing.

Key Points

  • Extended home isolation can cause severe psychological stress and mental health deterioration
  • Need to balance infection control measures with comprehensive worker health and wellness support
  • Importance of establishing robust mental health support systems in workplaces and society
