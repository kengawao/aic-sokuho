スポンサーリンク

Japan has seen a record number of childcare facility closures and bankruptcies between January and August 2024. The collapse stems from declining enrollment due to demographic shifts, severe staffing shortages, and rising operational costs. This paradoxically worsens the childcare shortage in urban areas while rural centers struggle with insufficient demand, exposing systemic weaknesses in how Japan supports working families.

Key Points

  • Declining birthrates reduce enrollment, making fixed operational costs unsustainable for many facilities
  • Wage competition and daycare worker shortages drive up labor costs while revenue stagnates
  • Sudden closures force parents into emergency childcare searches, threatening their employment stability
