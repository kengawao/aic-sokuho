Japan's official census data released in 2025 shows the population has fallen to approximately 122.97 million, marking a decline of about 3.17 million from the 2020 census. This represents the largest decrease since records began. The rapid population decline poses serious challenges including labor shortages, economic contraction, and mounting pressures on social security systems.

Key Points

  • A loss of 3.17 million people over five years equals nearly 3% population decline, an unprecedented rate of decrease
  • Population decline varies dramatically by region, with rural areas experiencing severe demographic collapse
  • Simultaneous decrease in working-age population and increase in elderly dependency creates compounding economic and social security challenges
