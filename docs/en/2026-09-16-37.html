JR West has announced plans to preserve the Doctor Yellow (the distinctive yellow Shinkansen inspection train) following its retirement. This specialized vehicle, which monitored safety on the Tokaido Shinkansen, is being phased out due to advances in inspection technology and more efficient new systems. The announcement has sparked nostalgia among railway enthusiasts who cherished the unique presence of this beloved yellow train.
