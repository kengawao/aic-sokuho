Japan Sets Screen Time Limits on Digital Textbooks, Citing Student Health Concerns

国内 2026-09-03 • Source: デジタル教科書 動画時間に上限

スポンサーリンク

The Japanese Ministry of Education announced plans to introduce time restrictions on video content in digital textbooks. As digital textbooks are rapidly expanding in schools, officials are concerned about potential negative effects on students' eyesight and concentration from prolonged screen exposure. The move aims to balance educational effectiveness with student health. Specific time limits and implementation dates remain to be determined through further deliberation.

Key Points

  • The rapid expansion of digital textbooks raises health concerns about children's prolonged screen exposure
  • While videos enhance learning outcomes, managing total screen time has proven challenging
  • Japan must develop its own digital education guidelines while learning from international regulatory approaches
