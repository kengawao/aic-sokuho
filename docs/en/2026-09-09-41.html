Why is Apple Taking 10 Years to Develop a Foldable iPhone?

IT 2026-09-09 • Source: 折り畳み式iPhone 開発に約10年

スポンサーリンク

Apple is reportedly spending approximately 10 years developing a foldable iPhone, according to recent reports. Rather than rushing to market like competitors, Apple is addressing multiple technical challenges including screen durability, hinge mechanisms, and software optimization. While Samsung Galaxy and other competitors have already launched foldable devices, Apple's cautious approach reflects its strategy to differentiate through superior build quality and user experience.

Key Points

  • Foldable displays require solving numerous engineering challenges such as fold durability, hinge design, crease visibility, and OS optimization—suggesting Apple prioritizes complete technical solutions over early market entry
  • Apple's delayed timeline may be a deliberate branding strategy to avoid the quality issues seen with early competitors like Galaxy Fold, positioning a future Apple foldable as the 'definitive' version
  • Product decisions beyond engineering—including form factor choice (vertical vs. horizontal fold), market demand validation, and manufacturing cost viability—likely factor into the extended development timeline
