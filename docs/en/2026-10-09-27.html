Japanese Household Spending Tumbles for 9th Consecutive Month

経済 2026-10-09 • Source: 8月家計調査 実質の消費支出9か月連続減少

スポンサーリンク

Japan's August household consumption survey reveals a concerning trend: real consumer spending among households with two or more members declined 3.1% year-on-year. This marks the ninth consecutive month of decline in real terms. The persistent downturn, even after adjusting for inflation, points to deepening household purchasing power erosion amid a broader economic slowdown.

Key Points

  • Real consumption expenditure down for 9 straight months with no sign of recovery
  • Even after inflation adjustment, 3.1% decline shows households losing actual purchasing power
  • Wage growth failing to keep pace with price increases, forcing sustained consumer pullback
