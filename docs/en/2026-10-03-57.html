Can an App Solve Household Chore Inequality? Japanese City Tests 'Visible' Chore Sharing

国内 2026-10-03 • Source: アプリで家事分担を可視化 市実験

スポンサーリンク

A Japanese municipality is conducting an experiment using a smartphone app to visualize household chore distribution between spouses. The app allows couples to digitally record and share who performs which tasks and how much time is spent, aiming to create objective data about chore allocation. By making typically invisible domestic labor visible and measurable, the initiative seeks to facilitate more equitable discussions and reduce one-sided burden, particularly on women.

Key Points

  • Visualizing chore distribution through data could expose gender imbalances in household labor that are often overlooked or underestimated by family members
  • The practical effectiveness remains uncertain—whether app-mediated awareness will actually lead to behavioral change and more equitable arrangements between spouses
  • Connection to broader gender equality and workforce participation goals: if household burden is more equally shared, it could enable greater female participation in the labor market and professional development
