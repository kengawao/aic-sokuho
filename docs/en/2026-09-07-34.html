Will AI Answer Japan's Emergency Calls? How This Could Change 911 Response Forever

国内 2026-09-07 • Source: 110番通報対応のAI開発へ 警察庁

スポンサーリンク

Japan's National Police Agency is developing an AI system to assist with 110 emergency calls (Japan's equivalent to 911). The technology uses voice recognition to automatically record and classify caller information, accelerating handoff to responding officers. While this promises faster dispatch and more accurate priority assessment, concerns remain about AI misclassification leading to delayed response for serious crimes and privacy issues related to voice data storage.

Key Points

  • Voice recognition AI could automate call recording and classification, enabling faster information transfer to dispatchers and officers
  • Objective AI-based severity assessment may improve response prioritization, but risks exist of miscategorization leading to delayed response for critical incidents
  • Privacy and regulatory challenges must be addressed regarding voice data retention and use for AI model training under Japan's Personal Information Protection Act
