More than 1,700 gas stations have closed in Japan over the past decade, driven by EV adoption, declining gasoline demand, and deteriorating business profitability. The disappearance of fuel stations, particularly in rural areas, raises concerns about consumer convenience, regional economic impact, and the need for industry transformation toward charging infrastructure.

Key Points

  • Sharp decline in gasoline demand due to EV shift, exacerbating profitability issues for traditional gas station operators
  • Growing supply gaps in remote and rural regions, affecting residents' access to fuel and creating potential logistics challenges
  • Industry restructuring: transition to EV charging hubs and consolidation leaving small-to-medium operators behind
