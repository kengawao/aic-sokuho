How 'Easy Japanese' Became a Lifeline During the Kumamoto Earthquake: A Solution for Disaster Communication

国内 2026-09-01 • Source: やさしい日本語 熊本地震でも活用

スポンサーリンク

During the Kumamoto earthquake, 'Easy Japanese' proved to be an effective tool for communicating disaster information to foreign residents. This method simplifies language by using basic vocabulary, shorter sentences, and avoiding complex kanji, making it accessible not only to Japanese language learners but also to elderly people and the deaf community. Authorities have recognized Easy Japanese as a more cost-effective and practical alternative to multilingual responses for disaster management.

Key Points

  • Easy Japanese offers a low-cost, implementable alternative to multilingual crisis communication systems for local governments and public institutions
  • The approach simultaneously addresses the needs of multiple vulnerable groups—foreign nationals, elderly residents, and deaf/hard-of-hearing populations—through inclusive communication design
  • Scaling Easy Japanese across broadcast media, neighborhood associations, and social media platforms remains a critical challenge for disaster preparedness infrastructure development
