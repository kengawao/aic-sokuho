A troubling trend of internship programs offering wages as low as 100 yen per hour—far below Japan's minimum wage—has sparked debate about labor practices and corporate ethics. Companies justify these rates as 'learning opportunities' rather than employment, creating a legal gray area. Critics argue this system exploits students and exacerbates socioeconomic inequality in access to valuable career experience.
