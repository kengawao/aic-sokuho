Colombia Considers 'Prison Ships' to Combat Overcrowding and Drug Cartel Violence Behind Bars

国際 2026-08-28 • Source: コロンビア「監獄船」の導入検討

スポンサーリンク

Colombia is exploring the use of floating prison facilities, or 'prison ships,' to address severe overcrowding in its correctional system. The country faces escalating violence from drug trafficking organizations battling for control within prisons, overwhelming existing infrastructure. By dispersing inmates across maritime detention facilities, authorities aim to reduce congestion and contain gang-related violence. However, human rights organizations have raised serious concerns about potential abuses and inhumane conditions.

Key Points

  • Drug cartel clashes in overcrowded prisons have led to frequent riots and violence beyond government control
  • Offshore facilities would distribute inmates and ease overcrowding, but raise significant human rights and welfare concerns
  • International legal precedents and compliance with human rights law will be critical implementation challenges
