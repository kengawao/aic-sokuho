スポンサーリンク

Asia Games organizers are moving away from the traditional dominance of major advertising agencies, instead adopting a multi-vendor approach that includes regional and smaller firms. This shift aims to improve transparency and cost efficiency, marking a significant departure from the longstanding practice of concentrating projects with large agencies. The change is expected to reshape procurement practices for future public events.

Key Points

  • Transition from single-vendor monopoly to competitive multi-vendor bidding increases transparency and reduces costs
  • Expanded opportunities for regional SMEs and local businesses to participate, boosting local economic impact
  • Procurement reform for mega-events may set a precedent for future government contract practices across sectors
