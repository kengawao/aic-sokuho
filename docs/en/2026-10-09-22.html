Work-related mental health disability claims in Japan's healthcare industry have surged threefold, raising serious concerns about workplace conditions. Prolonged shifts, harassment, and high-stress patient interactions are taking a toll on doctors, nurses, and other medical staff. The crisis highlights an urgent need for systemic improvements in labor practices and mental health support within the healthcare sector.
