When Teachers Are Missing: Japan's Public Schools Face Staffing Crisis

国内 2026-09-02 • Source: 公立小中で「教員不足」深刻 実態

スポンサーリンク

Public elementary and junior high schools across Japan are experiencing a severe shortage of teachers. The inability to secure substitute staff for those on medical leave or parental leave is causing disruptions in class schedules and cancellations of school events. Declining birth rates and decreasing numbers of teacher certification candidates are creating a structural challenge, with mounting workloads on remaining staff perpetuating the crisis cycle.

Key Points

  • Schools struggle to find substitute teachers for medical and parental leave, disrupting normal class operations
  • Fewer candidates taking teacher recruitment exams reflects declining interest in the profession
  • Teacher overwork and inadequate compensation create a vicious cycle of declining career interest
