JDI's Factory Sale Marks Japan's Shift from LCD to Semiconductors

経済 2026-10-01 • Source: 経営再建中のJDI 茂原工場を半導体部品メーカーに売却決定

スポンサーリンク

Japan Display Inc., undergoing restructuring, has decided to sell its Mobara factory in Chiba Prefecture to a semiconductor components manufacturer based in Nagano Prefecture. The plant ceased production in November 2024 as the LCD panel market contracted. This transaction reflects Japan's industrial transformation driven by declining smartphone and TV LCD demand and rising semiconductor needs. Asset repositioning has become critical as the company pursues turnaround efforts.

Key Points

  • JDI's crisis background: LCD panel market saturation and reduced demand from smartphones and TVs forced multiple layoffs. The Mobara facility shutdown in November 2024 represents a strategic exit from mature LCD production.
  • Shift to semiconductor components: The buyer's use of the facility offers potential for infrastructure reuse and local employment continuation, supporting Japan's semiconductor revitalization strategy against industry hollowing.
  • Challenges in transition: Technical compatibility of LCD manufacturing equipment with semiconductor production, workforce reskilling needs, and concrete employment guarantees remain critical success factors for this industrial transformation.
