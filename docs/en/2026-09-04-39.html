Sanseido Bookstore Ikebukuro Main Store to Close After 70+ Years of History

経済 2026-09-04 • Source: 三省堂書店池袋本店 12/31閉店へ

スポンサーリンク

Sanseido Bookstore has announced the closure of its flagship Ikebukuro location on December 31, 2024. The store, which has operated for over 70 years, has long served as a cultural landmark in Ikebukuro. The closure reflects the deteriorating management environment for bookstores, driven by Japan's publishing downturn and the rapid shift to digital reading formats and online retail.

Key Points

  • The successive closures of major bookstores symbolize structural challenges facing Japan's entire publishing industry, with the number of bookstores nationwide cut in half over the past two decades
  • The shift in reading habits toward digital formats and intensifying competition from online retailers have eroded the profitability of physical bookstores
  • Skyrocketing commercial rents and labor costs in urban areas like Ikebukuro, combined with the declining viability of the large-format urban bookstore model
