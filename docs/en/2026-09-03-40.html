Hikone Castle Recommended as Japan's World Heritage Candidate—Could Become Shiga's First UNESCO Site

国内 2026-09-03 • Source: 世界文化遺産の国内候補に「彦根城」推薦決定 文化庁の審議会

スポンサーリンク

Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs has officially recommended Hikone Castle in Shiga Prefecture as a domestic candidate for UNESCO World Heritage status, targeting registration in 2027. Designated as a National Treasure with well-preserved Edo-period architecture, successful registration would make it Shiga Prefecture's first World Heritage site and require approval through ICOMOS evaluation before final UNESCO consideration.

Key Points

  • Cultural and historical significance: Hikone Castle is valued as an outstanding example of Edo-period castle architecture, representing Japan's feudal heritage with exceptional preservation and documented construction history
  • UNESCO registration process timeline: Approximately two years will pass between domestic recommendation and UNESCO review, during which the International Council on Monuments and Sites (ICOMOS) will conduct on-site inspection and technical evaluation
  • Regional economic and tourism impacts: Expected increase in international visitors and tourism revenue, balanced against the need for sustainable management strategies and infrastructure improvements to prevent overtourism
