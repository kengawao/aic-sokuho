The Bank of Japan is expected to raise its policy interest rate to around 1.25% during its monetary policy meeting, accelerating the pace of rate hikes in response to mounting inflation risks. The decision signals increased concerns about persistent price rises and their impact on the economy. The higher rates are likely to be passed on to consumers through increased mortgage and consumer loan rates, raising questions about the effects on household finances.
