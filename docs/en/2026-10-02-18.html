スポンサーリンク

Ahead of Japan's adoption of the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS)—a system to verify whether individuals working with children have criminal records—the Cabinet Office for Children and Families has opened a consultation window for business operators. The system checks sexual crime histories of those seeking positions in child-related facilities, following models already established in Western countries. The measure aims to prevent child abuse and exploitation through strict vetting procedures.

Key Points

  • Japan's DBS system is modeled on the UK's version implemented since the 1990s, designed to assess the suitability of child-sector workers through background checks
  • The consultation window enables schools, daycare centers, and sports organizations to clarify operational procedures and address concerns before full implementation, ensuring smoother rollout
  • Balancing strict management of criminal records with individual privacy protection is critical; how this equilibrium is struck will determine the system's practical effectiveness
