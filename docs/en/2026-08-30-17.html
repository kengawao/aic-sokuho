Researchers have confirmed breeding populations of Asiatic black bears (Ursus thibetanus) in Shikoku, a Japanese region where the species was thought to have disappeared in the 1950s. Recent sightings and ecological evidence suggest the species is reestablishing itself in the region. This development raises complex questions about wildlife management, human-wildlife coexistence, and the ecological shifts occurring across rural Japan.
