G7 Agrees to Release 100 Million Barrels After Trump's Export Ban Threat

国際 2026-10-03 • Source: G7が石油備蓄１億バレルを協調放出へ　トランプ氏が輸出禁止を脅した後

スポンサーリンク

The G7 agreed to collectively release a total of 100 million barrels of oil reserves over four months through the International Energy Agency to combat soaring fuel prices. The announcement came shortly after U.S. President Trump hinted at a potential diesel export ban. French President Macron, serving as G7 chair, announced the decision following the virtual meeting.

Key Points

  • The G7's first major coordinated oil reserve release reflects serious concerns about persistent energy price surges affecting global economies
  • Trump's export ban threat may have accelerated G7's decision-making and demonstrated the group's ability to respond to external pressure
  • The actual market impact of the 100 million barrel release will depend on how financial markets and OPEC respond to this supply increase signal
