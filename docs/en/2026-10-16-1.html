Major Healthcare Policy Shift Coming in August 2029: Financial Income Now Counts Toward Medical Expenses

経済 2026-10-16 • Source: 金融所得を医療費に反映 29年8月

スポンサーリンク

Starting in August 2029, Japan will incorporate financial income into the calculation of out-of-pocket medical expenses. Previously, medical fee assessments were based primarily on salaries and business income, but investment gains, stock dividends, and forex profits will now be factored in. This change is expected to increase medical costs for asset-holding individuals and affect multiple government assistance programs including high-cost medical expense coverage.

Key Points

  • Financial income inclusion could significantly raise medical expenses for high-income investors and dividend earners
  • Stock dividends, capital gains, and other investment income previously excluded from medical fee calculations will now be considered
  • Changes to high-cost medical expense assistance and other subsidy programs are anticipated
