Why Are Landlords Rejecting Guide Dog Users? Japan Launches National Investigation into Housing Discrimination

国内 2026-10-08 • Source: 盲導犬との賃貸入居拒否 国調査へ

スポンサーリンク

Cases of visual impairment residents being denied rental housing because of their guide dogs are increasing across Japan. Although the Physical Disability Assistance Dog Law mandates public facility access, private rental properties have weaker legal enforcement. The government has initiated a fact-finding survey to address the gap between legal protection and real-world barriers. The challenge lies in balancing disability rights with property owners' concerns.

Key Points

  • The Physical Disability Assistance Dog Law requires guide dog acceptance, yet rental property rejections continue due to weak enforcement
  • Property owners cite 'no pets' policies and damage concerns, while the law lacks binding force in private sectors
  • Japan's first government investigation aims to gather evidence and may lead to stronger regulations and public awareness campaigns
