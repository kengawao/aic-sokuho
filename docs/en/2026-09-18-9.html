Growing numbers of children are experiencing a condition where they cannot speak in specific environments like schools. This symptom is part of an anxiety disorder that cannot be overcome through willpower alone, but rather has neurobiological roots. Experts emphasize that early detection and appropriate professional support are crucial, while raising awareness among parents and educators remains a major challenge.

Key Points

  • The inability to speak in certain settings is an anxiety-related condition beyond the child's voluntary control
  • Early diagnosis and professional treatment intervention leads to significant improvement
  • Lack of understanding from parents and teachers can worsen the child's psychological state
