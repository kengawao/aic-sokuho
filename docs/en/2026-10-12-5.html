国内 2026-10-12 • Source: 新築の平屋率が増加 耐震性に注目

スポンサーリンク

The proportion of newly built single-story homes in Japan is on the rise, driven primarily by renewed interest in earthquake resistance. Single-story homes are gaining recognition for their structural stability and reduced seismic vulnerability. Additionally, their appeal is boosted by the aging society, as they offer barrier-free living spaces and accessibility without stairs. These practical advantages are reshaping homebuyer preferences in a seismically active nation.

Key Points

  • Single-story homes experience less seismic motion and are increasingly recognized for superior earthquake resilience
  • Barrier-free design without stairs is becoming essential for an aging population
  • Efficiency in construction and maintenance costs also contributes to the growing preference for single-story homes
