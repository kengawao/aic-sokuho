Japan's economy is reportedly expanding, yet the average citizen hardly notices any improvement. While weak yen boosts export companies' profits and stock prices climb, inflation outpaces wage growth, eroding real purchasing power. Essential goods have become significantly more expensive, creating a stark divide between wealthy corporate shareholders and ordinary workers who fail to benefit from the supposed economic expansion.

Key Points

  • Weak yen inflates export company earnings but increases import costs, burdening consumers
  • Inflation rate surpasses wage growth, causing real purchasing power to decline across income levels
  • Economic gains concentrate among financial sector and large corporations, while working families experience stagnant wages and rising living costs
