Why Telecom Carriers Are Struggling with Customer 'Hopping'

経済 2026-08-11 • Source: 携帯キャリア悩ませるホッピング

スポンサーリンク

Japanese mobile carriers are facing a growing challenge from 'hopping'—frequent customer switching between carriers. Historically, customers remained loyal to a single carrier, but deregulation of SIM cards and improvements to MNP (Mobile Number Portability) have drastically lowered switching barriers. As carriers compete intensely for new customers, acquisition costs are rising while retention rates fall, creating structural pressure on industry profitability.

Key Points

  • SIM deregulation and MNP improvements have significantly reduced switching friction for consumers
  • Diversified pricing plans and carrier differentiation encourage price-conscious users to frequently optimize their choices
  • Rising customer acquisition costs threaten carrier profitability as short customer lifespans fail to justify initial investment
