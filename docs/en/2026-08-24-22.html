The Japanese government announced plans to mandate automakers equip vehicles with systems to detect drowsy driving and distracted driving. Since lack of attention accounts for approximately 70% of traffic accidents, new vehicles will gradually adopt this technology. These systems detect driver fatigue and distraction, then issue warnings to reduce serious accidents.

Key Points

  • Drowsy driving detection systems will be phased in starting with new vehicle models
  • Inattention is a leading cause of traffic accidents, and automatic detection is expected to reduce severe crashes
  • Implementation challenges remain regarding manufacturer costs and safety disparities with existing vehicles
