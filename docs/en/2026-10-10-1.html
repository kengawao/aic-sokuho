Skyticket Data Breach Affects 14.64 Million Users - Is Your Information at Risk?

IT 2026-10-10 • Source: スカイチケット 1464万件情報流出

スポンサーリンク

A major data breach at online flight booking platform Skyticket has exposed approximately 14.64 million customer records. The leaked information includes names, email addresses, and phone numbers, with the company attributing the incident to unauthorized external access. The company is notifying affected users and implementing enhanced security measures.

Key Points

  • The scale of the breach (14.64 million records) ranks among Japan's largest data incidents
  • Leaked data includes personally identifiable information that could facilitate secondary fraud and phishing attacks
  • Increased risk of identity theft, impersonation fraud, and unsolicited contact targeting affected users
