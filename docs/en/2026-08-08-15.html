Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism has officially designated the Shikoku Shinkansen as a study-targeted route. This marks a significant step toward potential realization, moving the project from the conceptual stage to formal feasibility assessment. However, multiple hurdles remain before construction, including route finalization, ridership forecasting, and funding mechanisms.

Key Points

  • The Shikoku Shinkansen has been officially selected for study, advancing the project toward concrete deliberations on viability and implementation
  • While the region anticipates improved transportation networks and economic revitalization, challenges around financial sustainability and local government contributions persist
  • Actual construction requires overcoming several obstacles: finalizing the route, conducting detailed demand projections, and establishing a national subsidy framework
