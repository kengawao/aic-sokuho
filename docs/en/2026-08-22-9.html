Following a deadly flooding incident at an underpass in Chiba that claimed a man's life, Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism conducted emergency inspections of drainage systems nationwide. The inspection revealed that backup power systems failed to activate and drainage pumps did not function at multiple locations. This has exposed critical gaps in maintenance and safety management, prompting calls for comprehensive reforms to underground passage protection systems.

Key Points

  • Inadequate maintenance protocols and safety management systems for underpass drainage facilities require immediate overhaul
