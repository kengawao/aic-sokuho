SBI Holdings announced its acquisition of Livedoor, marking a symbolic moment in Japan's IT industry history. The deal comes over two decades after the infamous rivalry between Livedoor founder Takaaki Horie and Masayoshi Son of SoftBank dominated business headlines. The acquisition raises questions about how SBI will integrate Livedoor's portal site and legacy assets into its expanding financial and digital services empire.
