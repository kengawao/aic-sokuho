Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare announced on August 28 that influenza has entered a nationwide epidemic phase, with reported cases exceeding one patient per medical facility. This August declaration is extraordinarily early, as flu typically peaks from November onward. Experts point to climate change, viral mutations, and behavioral shifts as potential drivers of the earlier-than-usual outbreak timing.
