Archaeologists discovered arrows believed to be from the defeated Song Dynasty in submarine ruins off Nagasaki Prefecture, where ships from the Mongol invasions of Japan (Genko) sank during the Kamakura period. The find suggests the Yuan Dynasty strategically repurposed weapons from conquered territories. Research teams from the Nara National Research Institute for Cultural Properties are investigating how the Mongol Empire managed military resources, potentially shedding new light on 13th-century Asian military tactics and supply systems.

  • The material evidence contributes to understanding 13th-century inter-imperial commerce and military technology transfer across East Asia from an archaeological perspective
