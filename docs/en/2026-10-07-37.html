Unauthorized access incidents are surging across Japan, and cybersecurity experts are pointing to multiple culprits. The widespread adoption of remote work has exposed cracks in office-centric security systems, weak password management practices persist, and many organizations are lagging in their security investments and training. Experts stress that tackling this crisis requires more than just employee awareness—systemic organizational changes are essential.

Key Points

  • Remote work expansion has left many companies with outdated security infrastructure that wasn't designed for distributed workforces
  • Password reuse remains the primary attack vector, while multi-factor authentication adoption rates fall short of expectations
  • Organizations are falling behind on security investment and talent development, creating a widening gap in incident response capabilities
