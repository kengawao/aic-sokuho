As Japan grapples with aging demographics and population decline, more families are choosing to abandon family graves, a practice known as 'hakajimmai.' However, the disposal of discarded tombstones lacks clear legal frameworks, creating challenges around waste management costs and environmental concerns. Temples and stone craftsmen are struggling to cope with this growing trend.
