Amid rising interest rates and inflation concerns, increasing numbers of young Japanese homebuyers are accelerating purchase decisions. However, experts warn that purchasing without adequate savings or a solid long-term financial plan poses serious risks. Financial institutions are intensifying their cautionary stance against lending beyond borrowers' true repayment capacity.
