An increasing number of companies are facing bankruptcy primarily due to employee resignations, reaching record pace levels. Deteriorating work environments, inadequate compensation and benefits, and strained relationships with management are causing critical loss of human capital that directly impacts business viability. The issue highlights how talent retention has become a decisive factor in corporate survival during Japan's demographic decline.

Key Points

  • Workplace conditions and compensation reform are becoming essential factors for business continuity
  • Companies must adapt talent retention strategies to survive in an aging, shrinking population economy
