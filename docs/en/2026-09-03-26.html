A growing number of younger Japanese are pursuing FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early), with many targeting their 30s as the goal age. As interest in stock and index fund investments rises, both the feasibility and risks of early retirement are being scrutinized. The movement reflects Japan's shift toward individual wealth management amid stagnant wage growth and pension concerns.

Key Points

  • Increasing investor base among those in their 20s-40s, particularly targeting FIRE milestones
  • Diversified asset-building strategies emerging amid low interest rates and inflation
  • Expert opinions diverge on both achievability and potential pitfalls of early retirement plans
