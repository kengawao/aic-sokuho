Why Are Gas Stations Disappearing? The Rapid Industry Collapse Behind Closures

経済 2026-10-06 • Source: 倒産・廃業でガソスタ急速減 背景

スポンサーリンク

The number of gasoline stations across Japan is rapidly declining due to bankruptcies and closures. Multiple factors are contributing: structural decrease in fuel demand from EV adoption, volatile fuel prices squeezing margins, labor shortages, and self-service operation challenges. The trend represents a significant industrial shift worth monitoring as Japan transitions to electric mobility.

Key Points

  • EV and hybrid vehicle proliferation is structurally reducing gasoline demand over time
  • Volatile fuel prices severely pressure operations, especially for small independent stations
  • Staff shortages and rising labor costs make self-service conversions increasingly difficult
  • Geographic disparities are widening, with closures accelerating in less profitable rural areas
