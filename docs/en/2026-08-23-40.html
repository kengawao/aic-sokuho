Experts warn that such obsessive thinking can significantly interfere with daily life and social functioning.

Key Points

  • Excessive body odor anxiety often occurs independently of actual odor and is primarily driven by subjective perception and cognitive bias
  • This psychological state shows connections to anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders, with distorted thinking patterns as the underlying cause
  • The proliferation of odor-related content on social media has created an environment where everyone becomes hypersensitive to personal hygiene concerns
