Why Is Japan's Apparel Industry in Crisis? Understanding the Wave of Bankruptcies

経済 2026-10-11 • Source: アパレル製造・卸で倒産増加 背景

スポンサーリンク

The apparel manufacturing and wholesale sector in Japan is experiencing rising bankruptcy rates. Key factors include shifts in consumer purchasing behavior, the explosive growth of e-commerce platforms, difficulty for SMEs to adapt to online channels, excess inventory issues, and cost pressures from the weak yen and rising raw material expenses. Even major companies are now facing management difficulties, indicating deep structural challenges across the entire industry.

Key Points

  • Small and medium-sized manufacturers unable to adapt to e-commerce platforms are being eliminated from the market
  • Industry struggles with rapidly changing consumer preferences and production systems that cannot keep pace
  • Weak yen and material cost inflation are eroding profit margins across the board
