During his playing career, Harimoto established an all-time record of 3,085 hits, earning him the nickname 'Hit-Making Machine.' Key Points

  • His career as a broadcast analyst was equally distinguished, with his unique evaluative style and 'Katsu!' catchphrase resonating with baseball fans across generations
  • His passing marks the symbolic end of an era in Japanese baseball that spanned from the Showa through Heisei periods
