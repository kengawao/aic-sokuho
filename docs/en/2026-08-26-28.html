Hometown Tax Return Program Pushes 19 Prefectures into Deficit—Systemic Cracks Widening

経済 2026-08-26 • Source: ふるさと納税「赤字」19都府県

スポンサーリンク

Nineteen prefectures in Japan have fallen into deficit due to the hometown tax return (furusato nozei) program, which allows taxpayers to redirect their tax payments to municipalities of their choice. The outflow of tax revenue combined with rising return gift costs is straining local government budgets. Heated competition for higher return rates and unequal distribution of funds across regions have exposed fundamental flaws in the system's sustainability.

Key Points

  • 19 prefectures in deficit: Urban tax revenue drain exacerbates regional inequality
  • Escalating return gift competition: Rising costs from premium gifts strain municipal budgets
  • Structural design issues: Concentrated donations and inadequate regulatory oversight threaten program viability
