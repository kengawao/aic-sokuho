Sagawa Express Raises Delivery Fees by Average 13% – How Much Will Your Shipping Cost Increase?

Sagawa Express, one of Japan's major delivery services, announced a 13% average price increase on parcel deliveries. The rate hike is attributed to rising fuel costs, labor shortage pressures, and increased expenses from workplace improvements. The new pricing will soon take effect, potentially impacting household budgets and e-commerce businesses. Industry observers are watching to see if competitors like Yamato and Japan Post will follow suit.

Key Points

  • A 13% average increase directly impacts consumer shipping costs, particularly affecting online shoppers and business users
  • Rising fuel prices and labor shortage-driven wage increases are driving costs up across the logistics industry
  • Other major carriers' responses could trigger industry-wide price hikes, affecting e-commerce pricing structures
