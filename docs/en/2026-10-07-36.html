Railroad operators in Japan are losing their jobs due to declining eyesight, revealing a serious industry-wide challenge. The rail industry enforces strict vision standards for operators, and those who fail to meet these standards face immediate job loss. The aging workforce combined with severe personnel shortages has created a crisis, prompting calls for better reassignment and reemployment support programs.

Key Points
