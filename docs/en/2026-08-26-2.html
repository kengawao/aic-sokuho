Before the New School Term: What SOS Signs Should Parents Watch For?

国内 2026-08-26 • Source: 新学期前 子どものSOSサイン注意

スポンサーリンク

As the new school year approaches, children often experience stress from new environments and transitions. Experts urge parents to recognize warning signs such as school refusal, sleep disturbances, and loss of appetite. Early parental awareness of behavioral changes can help prevent bullying and school non-attendance issues.

Key Points

  • The period before and after school reopening is when children are most vulnerable to stress, and behavioral changes become critical signals
  • Physical symptoms like school refusal, insomnia, and poor appetite are often overlooked by parents
  • Early detection and dialogue between parent and child are key to preventing situations from becoming severe
