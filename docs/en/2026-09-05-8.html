Why Major Izakayas Are Now Selling Alcohol for Under $1: The Psychology Behind the Price War

経済 2026-09-05 • Source: 大手居酒屋も100円台の酒 狙いは

スポンサーリンク

Major izakaya (Japanese pub) chains are expanding their ultra-low-priced alcohol offerings under $1. This shift reflects rising consumer price sensitivity amid inflation and corporate strategies to increase foot traffic. The intensifying competition in the budget segment reveals a strategic pivot from maximizing per-customer spending to maximizing customer frequency and volume.

Key Points

  • Consumer price sensitivity has surged under inflation, forcing even major chains to embrace budget-focused strategies
  • Sub-$1 pricing creates psychological purchase triggers to boost visit frequency, with profits recovered through other menu items
  • The move counters competition from both small izakayas and alternative venues like convenience stores offering cheap alcohol
