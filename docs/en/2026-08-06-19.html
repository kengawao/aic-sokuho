Rakuten Ichiba is planning to introduce an AI-powered automatic customer service system called 'AI Shopkeeper' to provide 24/7 store assistance. Traditionally limited to business hours, this system would handle customer inquiries and product recommendations through AI, reducing burden on sellers while enhancing customer experience. The initiative could significantly reshape how EC platforms manage staffing and leverage AI technology going forward.

Key Points

  • The success depends on whether AI can accurately handle customer needs during off-hours while maintaining seller efficiency—a delicate balance between automation and personalization
  • Small-scale individual sellers could benefit massively from reduced labor, but limitations arise with complex product customization and complaint handling where AI may fall short
  • Balancing platform operational cost reduction against seller satisfaction and customer retention risks; over-automation may alienate users who value human interaction
