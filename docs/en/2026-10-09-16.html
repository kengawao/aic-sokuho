Foreign Workers Increasingly Flee Rural Areas for Japanese Cities, Raising Regional Concerns

経済 2026-10-09 • Source: 外国人材 都市部への転職が増加

スポンサーリンク

Foreign workers in Japan are increasingly transferring from rural to urban areas, driven by higher wages, better employment conditions, and improved quality of life. This trend is intensifying labor shortages in rural industries such as manufacturing and agriculture. Employers in less developed regions face growing competition from urban centers and must develop new retention strategies as labor mobility restrictions ease.

Key Points

  • Accelerating regional depopulation as foreign workers concentrate in urban centers, threatening rural industrial sustainability
  • Expanding wage gaps between urban and rural areas highlight the need for comprehensive regional development and workplace improvement initiatives
  • As labor mobility increases, companies must reassess recruitment and retention strategies to compete in the emerging international labor market
