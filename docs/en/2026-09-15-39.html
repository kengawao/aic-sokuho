Japan to Expand Flat 35 Mortgage Discounts for Families: Will Used Homes Be Game-Changers?

経済 2026-09-15 • Source: 子育て世帯「フラット35」金利引き下げ拡大を検討へ 国交省

スポンサーリンク

Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism is expanding support for child-rearing households under the Flat 35 long-term fixed-rate mortgage program. The policy, previously limited to new home purchases, will be extended to used home acquisitions. The ministry has included necessary budget allocations in next fiscal year's budget request. This initiative aims to reduce housing acquisition barriers and improve living environments for families with children.

Key Points

  • Expanding eligibility from new to used homes could reach more child-rearing households and stimulate Japan's underdeveloped used housing market, promoting better stock circulation
  • The Flat 35's 35-year fixed rate appeals to risk-averse borrowers, but implementation details—such as interest rate reduction amounts and eligibility criteria (child age limits, income caps)—will be crucial for effectiveness
  • This represents government's use of housing policy as a low-birth-rate countermeasure, raising questions about coordination with other child support measures and fiscal efficiency
