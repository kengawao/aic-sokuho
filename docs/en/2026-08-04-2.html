Kumamoto Earthquake Relief: Over 730 Million Yen Raised Through Hometown Tax Donation Program

国内2026-08-04 | Source: 熊本地震 ふるさと納税7.3億円超

Donations exceeding 730 million yen have been collected through Japan's hometown tax program (furusato nozei) to support the Kumamoto earthquake disaster recovery. This substantial support reflects the combination of tax incentive benefits and strong public sympathy for affected areas. Notably, an increasing portion of donations are motivated by genuine support rather than return gifts offered by municipalities.

Key Points

  • The hometown tax donation system is proving to be an important mechanism for disaster relief funding
  • Tax deductions combined with social contribution motivations drive donation participation
  • Private sector fundraising plays a crucial role in disaster recovery alongside government resources
