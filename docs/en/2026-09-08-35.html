Mount Anak Krakatau volcano in Indonesia continues to erupt, forcing seven airports nationwide to close for two consecutive days on the 7th. The volcanic ash disruption led to approximately 3,000 flight cancellations, stranding over 340,000 people across the country. Major airports, including Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta International Airport, gradually resumed operations from the 8th onwards.
