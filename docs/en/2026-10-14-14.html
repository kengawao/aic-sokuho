Japanese Textbooks to Face Page Limits for First Time in 70 Years—Will This Ease Students' Burden?

国内 2026-10-14 • Source: 教科書ページ上限設定へ 70年ぶり

スポンサーリンク

Japan's Ministry of Education is set to introduce regulations limiting the number of pages in textbooks, marking the first regulatory change in approximately 70 years since 1953. Textbook volume has expanded significantly over the decades, creating increased burden on students. With the rise of digital learning materials, authorities aim to balance educational content enrichment with student workload. This reform is expected to align with new curriculum guidelines starting next academic year.

Key Points

  • Textbook page counts have grown substantially over seven decades, creating significant physical and cognitive load on students that has become a recognized issue
  • Advances in digital educational tools now make it technically feasible to reduce paper-based content while maintaining comprehensive learning materials
  • Schools and publishers must adapt to these changes; maintaining educational quality while working within page limits will be a critical challenge
