According to the driver's statement to Tobu Railway, he received a signal confirming that all workers had evacuated before entering the platform. However, immediately upon entry, he spotted workers still on the tracks and applied emergency braking, but was unable to stop in time. The incident raises serious questions about safety procedures and communication clarity during trackside maintenance operations.
