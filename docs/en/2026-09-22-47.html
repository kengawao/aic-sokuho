Aging in Japan: Why Elderly Struggle to Rent Homes

国内 2026-09-22 • Source: 家借りられない高齢者も 背景は

スポンサーリンク

Japan faces a growing crisis as elderly citizens encounter difficulties securing rental housing. The challenges stem from the absence of guarantors, strict payment ability assessments by landlords, and widespread landlord anxiety about elderly tenants. This multifaceted issue demands urgent policy solutions as the nation's aging population continues to expand.

Key Points

  • Elderly renters without family guarantors face systematic contract rejection
  • Landlords' risk-averse policies push elderly applicants to the bottom of priority lists
  • Financial stability alone doesn't guarantee housing access for seniors
