Decarbonization Strategy Backfires: Why Going Green Is Making Us More Dependent on China

経済 2026-10-09 • Source: 進む脱炭素 中国依存拡大のリスク

スポンサーリンク

As the world accelerates its shift toward decarbonization, a critical vulnerability has emerged: the renewable energy supply chain is becoming increasingly concentrated in China. Solar panels, EV batteries, and rare earth minerals—all essential for net-zero targets—are now dominated by Chinese manufacturers and miners. This unintended consequence raises serious geopolitical and economic security concerns, forcing developed nations to rethink their climate strategies and balance environmental goals with supply chain resilience.

Key Points

  • Critical materials for green technology—solar panels, lithium-ion batteries, rare earths—are now heavily concentrated in China, creating supply chain bottlenecks
  • China's control over key decarbonization industries amplifies geopolitical risks including potential supply disruptions, price manipulation, and asymmetric negotiating power
  • Advanced economies' push for decarbonization paradoxically strengthens China's economic influence and global strategic leverage
