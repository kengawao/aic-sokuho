Hankyu Railway Company announced it will discontinue hanging advertisements inside train cars starting April 2025. The decision reflects the widespread impact of smartphone adoption, as passengers increasingly focus on their mobile devices rather than observing traditional in-train advertisements. This shift signals a broader transformation in Japanese railway companies' advertising strategies.

Key Points

  • Smartphone penetration has shifted passenger attention downward, reducing the effectiveness of traditional hanging advertisements in transit environments
  • Railway operators are accelerating their pivot toward digital and video advertising platforms as part of broader strategic repositioning
  • Discontinuing hanging ads creates challenges including lost advertising revenue contracts and the need to diversify information delivery methods to passengers
