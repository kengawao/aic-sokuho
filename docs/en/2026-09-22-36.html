スポンサーリンク

A notable increase in bicycle accidents is reported during the spring school term when students advance to new grades. Experts attribute this to expanded travel ranges, relaxed vigilance, and inconsistent knowledge of traffic rules among different grade levels. Safety educators emphasize the importance of reinforced traffic safety instruction through school-family coordination and regular bicycle maintenance checks including brakes, tires, and lights. Comprehensive prevention strategies are becoming increasingly critical.

Key Points

  • Grade advancement leads to expanded travel radius and reduced parental supervision, which are primary factors in increased accident risk
  • Variations in traffic safety education content and effectiveness across grades create gaps in rule comprehension among students
  • Regular bicycle maintenance checks (brakes, tires, lights) combined with household safety guidance are essential for accident prevention
