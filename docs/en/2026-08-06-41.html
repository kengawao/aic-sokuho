Japan's Pachinko Industry in Crisis: Number of Operators Halves in a Decade

経済 2026-08-06 • Source: 「パチンコ」法人数 10年間で半減

スポンサーリンク

Japan's pachinko industry is facing severe decline as the number of licensed operators has approximately halved over the past decade. The contraction is driven by demographic aging and customer exodus, intensifying competition from digital entertainment alternatives like mobile games and streaming services, and regulatory pressures including stricter gambling addiction prevention measures. The sector, once considered a major pillar of Japan's entertainment economy, is undergoing fundamental structural transformation.

Key Points

  • Number of pachinko-related corporations has decreased by roughly 50% in 10 years, reflecting rapid industry shrinkage and consolidation
  • Aging population reducing customer base while smartphone gaming and streaming platforms intensify competition for leisure spending
  • Regulatory tightening on gambling addiction prevention and labor standards reforms are compressing operating margins across the sector
