Why Are Companies Expanding Employee Housing Benefits?

経済 2026-09-24 • Source: 社員寮や社宅が増加 企業の期待は

スポンサーリンク

Japanese companies are increasingly investing in employee dormitories and company housing. With wage increases becoming difficult, employers are using residential support as a key recruitment and retention tool. Changes in work styles following remote work adoption have also contributed to this trend.

Key Points

  • Housing benefits are emerging as a competitive advantage in talent acquisition when wage increases are constrained
  • Cost reduction in housing expenses directly improves quality of life for new employees and those relocated from rural areas
  • Companies view company housing as an effective tool for employee management and retention
