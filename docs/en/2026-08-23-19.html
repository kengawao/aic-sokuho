Japan's House of Representatives is considering the introduction of the Sainte-Laguë method, a new vote-to-seat allocation system for proportional representation. Proponents argue it could resolve issues with wasted votes and disproportionate seat distribution under the current system. However, the proposal has become a focal point for political debate, as different parties stand to gain or lose seats depending on the specific design, making consensus difficult.
