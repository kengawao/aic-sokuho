Japan's personal financial assets have reached an all-time high of 2,519 trillion yen, driven primarily by rising stock prices and weak yen effects. However, economists point out that most assets are concentrated among older generations, while younger people struggle to build wealth. This generational disparity in asset accumulation raises questions about Japan's economic future.

Key Points

  • Personal financial assets surpass 2.5 quadrillion yen for the first time, showing significant year-over-year growth
  • Stock market gains and currency depreciation are key drivers behind the asset increase
  • Severe intergenerational wealth gap: assets heavily skewed toward retirees while younger workers lag far behind
