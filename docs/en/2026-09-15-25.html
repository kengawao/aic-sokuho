Japan's Post-War Longest Economic Expansion Falls Flat: Only 15% Feel Prosperity

経済 2026-09-15 • Source: 景気よくなっている実感「ある」15％「ない」78％ NHK世論調査

スポンサーリンク

An NHK public opinion survey reveals a stark disconnect between official economic data and public sentiment. While Japan's economic expansion is said to be the longest since World War II, only 15% of respondents reported feeling an improvement in their economic situation, whereas 78% said they felt no such improvement. This gap highlights how widespread inflation and rising living costs may be offsetting wage increases and employment gains for most households.

Key Points

  • Significant gap between macroeconomic statistics and household-level economic sentiment
  • Inflation in essentials outpacing wage growth for majority of workers
  • Uneven distribution of economic benefits across regions and employment types
