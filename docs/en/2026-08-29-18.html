Why Are Some NISA Investors Getting Poorer? The Pitfall of 'Maximizing Tax-Free Limits'

経済 2026-08-29 • Source: 「NISA貧乏」極限の投資生活 実態

スポンサーリンク

A troubling trend has emerged where NISA investors are actually experiencing asset losses despite the tax-free advantages. Many novice investors are opening NISA accounts without adequate financial literacy, focusing solely on the tax benefits while ignoring proper risk management. A common mistake involves treating the annual tax-free contribution limit as an obligation to fill, leading to poorly considered investment decisions that ultimately result in losses.

Key Points

  • Investors prioritize tax benefits over sound investment fundamentals, forgetting that NISA is a tax-advantaged account, not a capital guarantee. Lack of basic financial literacy about market risk
  • Misunderstanding the annual contribution limit (120,000 yen for general NISA) as a quota to fulfill, resulting in unnecessary purchases and careless stock selection
  • Insufficient investment knowledge leading to inappropriate asset allocation—holding high-risk stocks or emerging market funds beyond personal risk tolerance. Absence of proper financial advisors and reliance on biased information sources
