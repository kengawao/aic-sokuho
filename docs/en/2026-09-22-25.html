Yuichiro Tamaki, leader of the Democratic Party for the People, visited Ukraine as the first opposition party leader from Japan to do so. During the war with Russia, Tamaki met with President Zelensky to demonstrate Japan's support and fulfill the opposition party's diplomatic role. The visit signals a shift toward bipartisan foreign policy engagement on this critical international issue.
