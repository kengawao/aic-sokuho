Mercari Bans Pokemon 30th Anniversary Card Listings Amid Reselling Crisis

経済 2026-09-16 • Source: メルカリ 30thポケカ一時出品禁止

スポンサーリンク

Mercari, Japan's leading marketplace app, has temporarily restricted listings for Pokemon Card 30th Anniversary products in response to rampant reselling and market disruption. Cards selling for several times their retail price and aggressive bulk purchasing have created accessibility issues for regular collectors, prompting the platform to take enforcement action.

Key Points

  • The restriction stems from cards being resold at 3-5x their retail price, with buy-and-hoard tactics dominating the secondary market
  • General consumers are being priced out of commemorative products, creating social pressure on both retailers and platforms to intervene
  • Platform-level sales restrictions raise questions about property rights and free commerce on marketplace services
