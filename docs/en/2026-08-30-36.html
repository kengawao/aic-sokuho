Moon to Get Its Own Time Zone, Independent From Earth

科学 2026-08-30 • Source: 月の時間決める動き 地球から独立

スポンサーリンク

International space agencies including NASA and the ESA are developing an independent timekeeping system for lunar operations. Since the Moon's rotation period differs significantly from Earth's 29.5-day cycle, and communication latency reaches 1.3 seconds one-way, a dedicated lunar time standard is necessary for efficient base operations and coordination. International standardization efforts are accelerating ahead of planned lunar base construction in the 2030s.

Key Points

  • The Moon's 29.5-day rotation period makes Earth's 24-hour time system impractical for lunar surface operations and scheduling
  • Communication delay of approximately 1.3 seconds between Earth and Moon makes real-time coordination difficult without an independent lunar timekeeping standard
  • Space agencies are collaborating to establish technical standards for 'Lunar Time' as multiple nations increase lunar exploration activities
