スポンサーリンク

Australia's record industry association announced it will exclude AI-generated or AI-majority-produced tracks from official music charts. The decision came after DJ Josh Fawaz's AI-created cover of Madonna's 'Like a Prayer' reached #1 on the dance singles chart and #2 overall. The move raises fundamental questions about how the music industry defines human creativity and artistic merit in the age of generative AI.

Key Points

  • The policy targets songs with AI as 'majority or entirety' of production—the line between partial and full AI use remains unclear
  • The chart success of an AI cover demonstrates commercial viability of algorithmic music, forcing industry response
  • This Australian precedent could influence international regulatory frameworks and reshape how streaming platforms classify AI-generated content
