Japan's Water Bills Keep Rising — But Can Households Take Any More?

経済 2026-09-05 • Source: 水道代の値上げ相次ぐ 家計を圧迫

スポンサーリンク

Water rates are increasing nationwide across Japan. Local authorities cite aging infrastructure requiring replacement and declining revenue from population loss as main reasons. Following price hikes in electricity and gas, this new burden on household finances marks another serious threat to family budgets.

Key Points

  • Costs for replacing aging water pipes and infrastructure maintenance are rapidly increasing
  • Structural problem: water revenues decline due to population decrease while infrastructure investment remains necessary
  • Impact on low-income households is particularly severe when essential utilities like water become more expensive
