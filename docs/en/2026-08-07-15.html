A major AI data center development is planned for Akita Prefecture in Japan. With AI computational demands consuming massive amounts of electricity, power supply security and efficient cooling capabilities have become the primary location criteria. Akita's abundant water resources and comparatively lower land costs offer significant advantages over congested urban areas like Tokyo. The project represents a potential turning point for rural revitalization and regional industrial development.
