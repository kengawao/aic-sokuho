Japan's Government to Investigate Animal Cafe Industry Over Welfare Concerns

国内 2026-09-02 • Source: アニマルカフェ 国が実態調査へ

スポンサーリンク

Japan's government has announced plans to conduct its first comprehensive investigation into animal cafes, which allow customers to interact with small animals like rabbits and hedgehogs. The survey will examine breeding conditions, handling time limits, and compliance with animal protection laws. The investigation aims to determine whether industry guidelines or legal reforms are needed to balance consumer satisfaction with animal welfare protections.

Key Points

  • Animal cafes currently operate in a legal gray zone with no established standards for animal care, handling duration, or facility conditions
  • While some facilities maintain high welfare standards, concerns persist about stress-related health issues and improper handling practices across the industry
  • The government faces pressure to develop enforceable guidelines that protect animal welfare while maintaining viable business operations, informed by international best practices
