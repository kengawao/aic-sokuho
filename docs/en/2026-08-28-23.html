Waitlisted Children Hit 2,435 in Japan—First Increase in 9 Years. Can We Solve the Childcare Shortage?

国内 2026-08-28 • Source: 全国の待機児童2435人 9年ぶり増

スポンサーリンク

Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare reported that the number of children on childcare facility waitlists has reached 2,435, marking the first increase in nine years. Despite ongoing efforts to eliminate waiting lists, fundamental challenges such as regional disparities and a critical shortage of childcare workers remain unresolved. The situation highlights the urgent need for comprehensive solutions to support working parents and boost childcare infrastructure.

Key Points

  • First increase in nine years signals a plateau or reversal in efforts to reduce waitlisted children
  • Severe regional disparities: major metropolitan areas struggle while some rural facilities face enrollment deficits
  • Childcare worker shortage remains the core issue: low wages and poor working conditions deter recruitment
