A combination of an autumn rain front and Typhoon 24 is bringing heavy rainfall across a wide area of Japan. Southwestern prefectures including Kagoshima, Miyazaki, Oita, and Kochi face particular risk of linear precipitation bands, which can produce extremely localized but intense downpours. Aomori Prefecture has already recorded record-breaking rainfall, with warnings continuing through September 7th as meteorologists urge maximum caution against landslides and flooding.

Key Points

  • Dual weather systems converging: autumn rain front combined with Typhoon 24 creating widespread heavy rainfall across southwestern Japan
  • Linear precipitation bands (sen-jō kōsuihatai) pose serious threat to Kagoshima, Miyazaki, Oita, and Kochi prefectures with potential for extreme localized flooding
  • Record rainfall already observed in Aomori with deteriorating conditions expected through September 7; residents urged to monitor forecasts and prepare evacuations
